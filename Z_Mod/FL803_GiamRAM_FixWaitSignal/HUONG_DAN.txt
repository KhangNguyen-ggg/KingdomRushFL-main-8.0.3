================================================
Ngay: 2026-10-22 | Base: commit 6e33473 (branch main, repo KhangNguyen-ggg/KingdomRushFL-main-8.0.3)

1. BAN VA NAY FIX GI?
- Roi ram do ham wait_signal trong all/systems.lua: moi lenh wait_signal dang ky 1
  closure vao signal registry toan cuc (hump.signal). Neu ban thoat / restart tran
  giua chung (truoc khi signal den), closure nay khong bao gio duoc go va van giu
  nguyen store cua tran cu -> ram tang lien tuc sau moi lan choi.
- Fix: (a) systems.lua theo doi cac handler da dang ky tren store;
  (b) game.lua xoa het cac handler con sot trong game:destroy();
  (c) main.lua chay GC manh hon (collectgarbage step 512) khi heap vuot 400 MB.

2. CACH CAI DAT (INSTALL)
- Giai nen ZIP nay ra mot thu muc tam.
- Chep DE noi dung thu muc CAP_NHAT vao goc game (thu muc chua main.lua va all/):
    CAP_NHAT/all/systems.lua -> all/systems.lua
    CAP_NHAT/all/game.lua    -> all/game.lua
    CAP_NHAT/main.lua        -> main.lua
- Truoc khi choi, kiem tra cu phap theo KIEM_TRA_LUA.txt.

3. CACH QUAY LAI BAN CU (ROLLBACK)
- Chep DE noi dung thu muc BACKUP vao goc game, cung vi tri nhu o muc 2.

4. LUU Y QUAN TRONG
- File .lua dung ky tu TAB de thut leu (KHONG phai khoang trang). Trinh soan thao
  co the tu dong doi TAB thanh space lam hong cu phap - kiem tra lai bang
  KIEM_TRA_LUA.txt sau moi lan chinh sua.
- Day la ban mod ca nhan: KHONG tai len Steam Workshop hoac pho bien cho nguoi khac.

5. KICH BAN TEST (5-10 phut)
- Mo Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) de xem RAM cua game.
- Vao tran, danh 1-2 wave roi THOAT ve menu giua chung (ESC -> Quit), vao lai
  tran moi. Lap lai 5-6 lan: RAM phai on dinh, khong tang dan.
- Test them: restart tran bang phim F5 (neu mod ho tro) lien tuc nhieu lan.
